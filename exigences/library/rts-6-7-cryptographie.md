# RTS 2024/1774 — articles 6 et 7 : chiffrement et gestion des clés
Source : règlement délégué (UE) 2024/1774, articles 6 « Encryption and cryptographic controls » et 7 « Cryptographic key management ». Extraits du texte officiel anglais ; vérifier sur EUR-Lex.

Article 6(1): financial entities shall develop, document, and implement a policy on encryption and cryptographic controls.
Article 6(2): the policy is designed on the basis of the results of an approved data classification and ICT risk assessment and includes rules for:
(a) the encryption of data at rest and in transit;
(b) the encryption of data in use, where necessary;
(c) the encryption of internal network connections and traffic with external parties;
(d) the cryptographic key management referred to in Article 7, laying down rules on the correct use, protection, and lifecycle of cryptographic keys.
Article 6(3): the policy includes criteria for the selection of cryptographic techniques and use practices, taking into account leading practices and standards.
Article 6(4): the policy includes provisions for updating or changing, where necessary, the cryptographic technology on the basis of developments in cryptanalysis.
Article 7(1): requirements for managing cryptographic keys through their whole lifecycle, including generating, renewing, storing, backing up, archiving, retrieving, transmitting, retiring, revoking, and destroying those cryptographic keys.
Article 7(2): Financial entities shall identify and implement controls to protect cryptographic keys through their whole lifecycle against loss, unauthorised access, disclosure, and modification.
Article 7(3): Financial entities shall develop and implement methods to replace the cryptographic keys in the case of loss, or where those keys are compromised or damaged.
