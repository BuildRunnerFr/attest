# RTS 2024/1774 — article 12 : journalisation
Source : règlement délégué (UE) 2024/1774, article 12 « Logging ». Extraits du texte officiel anglais ; vérifier sur EUR-Lex.

Article 12(1): financial entities shall develop, document, and implement logging procedures, protocols and tools.
Article 12(2): the logging procedures, protocols and tools shall contain all of the following:
(a) the identification of the events to be logged, the retention period of the logs […];
(c) the requirement to log events related to all of the following:
(i) logical and physical access control, as referred to in Article 21, and identity management;
(iii) change management;
(d) measures to protect logging systems and log information against tampering, deletion, and unauthorised access;
(e) measures to detect a failure of logging systems;
(f) the synchronisation of the clocks of each of the financial entity's ICT systems.
